How to Play basketball



Objective of the Game: Score more points than the opponent by shooting the ball through the hoop. Field goals inside the arc = 2 points; beyond the arc = 3 points; free throws = 1 point.

Basic Rules: Dribble to move — running while holding the ball is traveling. No double dribble — you cannot stop dribbling and start again. Stay in bounds — stepping on boundary lines with the ball stops play. Fouls occur with illegal physical contact.

Common Beginner Mistakes: Watching the ball while dribbling. Standing upright instead of staying in athletic stance. Rushing shots without proper form. Not using the non‑dribbling hand to protect the ball. Forgetting to move without the ball.
